# Acme Bank AI Governance Policy (v2.0)

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the governance framework under which Acme Bank designs,
procures, deploys, and operates artificial intelligence systems across the Group.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all AI systems used in production, including those developed in-house
and those procured from third parties, across all business lines.

## 3. AI System Inventory

All AI systems must be registered in the Acme Bank AI System Inventory prior to
deployment, with the following minimum metadata: system name, business owner,
intended purpose, data sources, and deployment date.

## 4. Governance Committee

The AI Governance Committee (AIGC) meets monthly and is the approval body for any
AI system classified as "high impact" under the Acme Bank AI Risk Tiering
Framework (see Section 5).

## 5. Risk Assessment Prior to Deployment

Before any AI system is deployed to production, the owning business unit must
complete a Pre-Deployment Risk Assessment covering:

- Intended purpose and use cases
- Data sources and data quality
- Known limitations of the model or system
- Fairness and bias considerations
- Sign-off from the AIGC for high-impact systems

## 6. Roles and Responsibilities

- **Chief AI Officer**: overall accountability for the AI governance framework
- **AI Governance Committee**: approval authority for high-impact systems
- **Business Unit Owner**: day-to-day operational accountability for each system
- **Model Risk Function**: independent review of model risk (see the separate
  Model Risk Management Standard)

## 7. Review Cadence

This policy is reviewed annually by the AI Governance Committee, or sooner if
required by material regulatory change.
