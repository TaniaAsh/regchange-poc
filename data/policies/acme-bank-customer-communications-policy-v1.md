# Acme Bank Customer Communications and Product Disclosure Policy (v1.0)

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets the standards for all communications and disclosures made to
customers regarding Acme Bank products and services, ensuring communications are
clear, fair, and not misleading.

## 2. Plain Language Standard

All customer-facing communications must be written in plain language, avoiding
unnecessary jargon, and must be reviewed by the Customer Experience team prior to
publication.

## 3. Product Disclosure Requirements

Product disclosure documents must include:

- A description of the product and its intended use
- Key risks associated with the product
- Fees and charges
- Contact details for customer support and complaints

## 4. Automated Decisioning Disclosures

Where a product decision (e.g. an application outcome) is materially influenced by
an automated system, the customer-facing communication must include a general
statement that automated processing was used and provide a route to request human
review, in line with existing consumer protection commitments.

## 5. Review and Approval

All new or materially changed customer communications require sign-off from
Legal and Compliance prior to publication.

## 6. Review Cadence

This policy is reviewed every 24 months by the Head of Customer Experience.
